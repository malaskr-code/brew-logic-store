Build me an online store called Brew Logic that
sells coffee machines and beans in Kuwait.
Slogan: "Engineer your cup".
Frontend only: no login, no database, no payments.
Use placeholder images. Do not copy photos from
other stores.

BRAND
- Personality: precise, warm, curious
- Logo: SVG line drawing of a V60 cone dripper
  next to the wordmark "Brew Logic"
- Fonts: Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for
  body, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic text

COLORS
Light mode (default):
- Background #F5EFE6, cards #FBF8F2
- Text #2B1D14
- Olive #6B7A3A for navbar, buttons, footer
Dark mode:
- Background #1C1410, cards #2A1F18
- Text #F2E8DA, accent #9AA85A

SWITCHES (both in the navbar)
- Light and dark theme switch
- English and Arabic language switch. English is
  the default. Arabic flips the layout to RTL.
  Keep brand and product names in English and
  translate everything else.

PAGES
- Home page (sections below)
- Machines page (/machines): only the machines
- Beans page (/beans): only the beans
Both category pages use the same product cards
as the home page. The navbar, cart drawer, theme,
and language work on every page.

HOME PAGE SECTIONS
1. Navbar: logo, Categories, Bundles, Contact,
   both switches, cart icon with item count.
   Categories is a dropdown with two options,
   Machines and Beans, each opening its page.
   On mobile the dropdown opens on tap.
2. Hero: headline "Engineer your cup", one line
   of text, Shop Now button that scrolls to
   the products
3. Products: grid with filter tabs All, Machines,
   Beans. Each card has an image, name, price in

4. Bundles: 3 cards. Each shows the included
   items, regular price crossed out, bundle

5. Cart drawer: items, quantity plus and minus,
   remove button, empty cart message, total in
   KWD, Checkout button
6. Floating WhatsApp button linking to
   https://wa.me/96598858879
7. Footer: Instagram link and contact email

MACHINES
- xBloom Studio, 199 KWD. Grinds, weighs, and
  pours in one machine, following a programmed
  recipe.
- Fellow Aiden Precision Coffee Maker, 129 KWD.
  Controls water temperature and pour timing
  for each stage of the brew.
- Moccamaster 10-Cup Coffee Maker, 100 KWD.
  A copper heating element holds water at
  brewing temperature from start to finish.
- Eureka Filtro grinder, 89 KWD. Flat steel
  burrs cut beans to an even size.
- Fellow Stagg EKG Electric Kettle Pro, 59 KWD.
  A PID controller holds water at the
  temperature you set.
- Hario V60 Drip Scale and Timer, 19 KWD.
  A load cell weighs the brew while a timer
  tracks the pour.
